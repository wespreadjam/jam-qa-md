# Notes that keep up with how you think

Quillmark is a note-taking app for people who think in outlines. Capture an idea in two keystrokes,
fold it into a project later, and find it again by meaning, not by the exact word you typed.

- **Outlines first.** Every note is a tree you can fold, drag and zoom into.
- **Backlinks without setup.** Mention a note and Quillmark links both ways.
- **Private by default.** Notes are end-to-end encrypted and work fully offline.

Quillmark runs on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android. Over 40,000 students, researchers and writers use it daily.

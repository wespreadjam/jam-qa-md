title: Backlinks explained, a beginner's guide
description: What backlinks are in a notes app and how they help you rediscover ideas.
---

A backlink is a link that points to the note you are reading. In Quillmark, when you link note A from
note B, note A shows B in its backlinks panel automatically.

Backlinks turn a pile of notes into a web. You stop asking "where did I put that?" and start asking
"what else did I write about this?".

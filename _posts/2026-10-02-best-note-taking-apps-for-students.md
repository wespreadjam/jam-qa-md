title: The best note-taking apps for students in 2026
description: A comparison of Quillmark, Notion, Obsidian and Apple Notes for students.
---

Students need three things from a notes app: speed in lectures, structure for revision, and a price that
fits a student budget. Here is how the popular options compare.

| App | Offline | Outlines | Student price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quillmark | Yes | Native | Free Pro |
| Notion | Partial | Toggle blocks | Free Plus |
| Obsidian | Yes | Plugin | Free |
| Apple Notes | Yes | No | Free |

Quillmark is the fastest for lecture capture; Obsidian is best if you want to own plain files.

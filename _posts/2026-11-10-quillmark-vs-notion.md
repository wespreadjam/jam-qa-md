Last updated: November 2026

Quillmark is the better fit if you think in outlines and want notes that stay private and work fully offline. Notion is the better fit if you need team databases, project tracking and built-in AI agents. Quillmark has end-to-end encrypted offline sync and costs $8/month for Pro; [Notion's Plus plan is $10 per member per month](https://www.notion.com/pricing), and [Notion is not end-to-end encrypted](https://notionmastery.com/security-and-privacy-concerns-in-notion).

## Which is better for outlining?

Quillmark is built around the outline. Every note is a foldable outline, so structure is the default rather than something you add.

Notion works with blocks. You can make a [toggle list with > and a space](https://www.notion.com/help/writing-and-editing-basics), but Notion's help says you open and close toggles manually, with no way to open or close them all at once or set them open by default. Notion's strength is the surrounding system: databases, sub-pages and connected apps.

## Which is better for privacy and offline use?

Quillmark syncs offline with end-to-end encryption, so only your devices can read your notes.

Notion encrypts traffic with [TLS 1.2 or higher and data at rest with AES-256](https://www.notion.com/help/security-and-privacy). It is not end-to-end encrypted: [Notion holds the keys](https://notionmastery.com/security-and-privacy-concerns-in-notion), and that article reports that Notion staff can see workspace content in support cases (the article dates from 2022, with a 2023 update).

Notion's offline mode is real, but it is page by page. [Anyone can download individual pages, and offline access is device-specific](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/working-offline-in-notion-everything-you-need-to-know), so you mark pages on each device. Only the first 50 rows of a database download automatically. On Business and Enterprise, Notion also downloads recent and favorited pages automatically.

## How do the prices compare?

| Plan level | Quillmark | Notion |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | [$0](https://www.notion.com/pricing) |
| Individual | Pro, $8/month | [Plus, $10 per member/month](https://www.notion.com/pricing) |
| Team | Teams, $14/user/month | [Business, $20 per member/month](https://www.notion.com/pricing) |

Notion also sells Enterprise, and [its pricing page lists Enterprise as custom pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) through its sales team. Notion's page says you can save up to 20% with yearly billing. Its page history differs by plan: 7 days on Free, 30 days on Plus and 90 days on Business. File uploads are up to 5 MB on Free and up to 5 GB on paid plans. Notion AI is a limited trial on Free and Plus; Business adds Notion Agent and AI Meeting Notes.

## Which is better for students?

Both are free for students. Quillmark gives students Pro free.

Notion's [Education Plan](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education) is free for students and teachers who sign in with a school email. It allows one member per workspace, up to 100 guests and 30 days of edit history. Students confirm their status with a school email once a year, and if that lapses the workspace moves to the Free plan. Upgrading that workspace to a paid plan permanently ends education eligibility.

## How do backlinks and search compare?

Both apps create backlinks automatically. In Notion, [backlinks appear when you @-mention a page](https://www.notion.com/help/create-links-and-backlinks) and are listed under the page title. You only see backlinks to pages you can access.

Quillmark adds semantic search across your notes on every plan. Notion's AI search across your workspace requires Notion AI, which is a limited trial on Free and Plus.

## Which is better for publishing?

Quillmark publishes notes as pages. Notion offers [1 published page on a notion.site domain on Free and 5 on Plus, Business and Enterprise](https://www.notion.com/pricing). Custom domains are charged per domain, at [$8 per month per domain paid annually or $10 monthly](https://www.notion.com/pricing).

## Who should pick which?

Pick Quillmark if:
- You write and research in outlines.
- You need notes that no server can read.
- You want one price of $8/month for Pro, or free Pro as a student.

Pick Notion if:
- You run a team that needs databases, permissions and SSO. [SAML SSO starts on Business](https://www.notion.com/pricing).
- You want AI agents working across your connected apps.
- You need shared project tracking more than a personal thinking tool.

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark a Notion alternative?**
For personal notes and outlining, yes. For team databases and project management, Notion does more.

**Does Notion work offline?**
Yes, on its desktop and mobile apps, for pages you download. Embeds, forms and buttons are unavailable offline, and you cannot share pages or change permissions while offline ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/guides/working-offline-in-notion-everything-you-need-to-know)).

**Is Notion end-to-end encrypted?**
No. Notion's own security exhibit describes TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest, and [an independent write-up](https://notionmastery.com/security-and-privacy-concerns-in-notion) confirms Notion keeps the keys.

**Is Notion free for students?**
Yes, through the [Education Plan](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education), with annual school email verification.

**What does Quillmark cost?**
Free, Pro at $8/month and Teams at $14/user/month. Students get Pro free.

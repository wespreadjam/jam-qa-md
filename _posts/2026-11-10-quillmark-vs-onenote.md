Pick Quillmark if you think in outlines and want notes that are end-to-end encrypted and fully usable offline. Pick OneNote if you want a free, free-form notebook with handwriting, Copilot and Microsoft 365 integration. Quillmark's [Free plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) costs $0 and Pro costs $8 a month, free for students. [OneNote is free](https://brandenbodendorfer.com/free-onenote-vs-microsoft-office-onenote) for basic note taking, with a Microsoft account needed for syncing.

## Is OneNote free, and what does Quillmark cost?

OneNote is free for basic note taking. [A Microsoft account is required for syncing](https://brandenbodendorfer.com/free-onenote-vs-microsoft-office-onenote), and Microsoft 365 is optional. Microsoft sells it inside Microsoft 365: [Personal at $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year (1 TB, one person), Family at $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year (2 TB shared, 1 to 6 people)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/onenote/digital-note-taking-app). Microsoft also advertises first-period offers, such as $6.99 for the first month of Personal. AI features on those plans are for the subscription owner only and have usage limits.

Quillmark has [three plans](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing):

- **Free, $0:** unlimited notes on 2 devices, outlines and backlinks.
- **Pro, $8 a month:** unlimited devices, version history, semantic search and publishing.
- **Teams, $14 per user per month:** shared spaces, admin controls, SSO and priority support.

Paid plans have a 14-day refund window. Students get Pro free with a school email and keep it until that email expires. Markdown and OPML export are available on every plan.

If cost is the only question, OneNote's free app is hard to beat. Quillmark's Free plan limits you to 2 devices, so syncing across a phone, laptop and tablet needs Pro, which is free for students.

## Which is more private: Quillmark or OneNote?

Quillmark [stores everything locally first and syncs in the background with end-to-end encryption](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/features).

OneNote notebooks are [encrypted in transit and at rest, but Microsoft holds the decryption keys](https://scrib.blog/blog/is-onenote-safe), according to a competing note app's analysis. OneNote does let you lock sections. [Passwords apply to sections only, not whole notebooks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onenote/protect-notes-with-a-password-in-onenote-for-windows-10). If you forget a password, [no one can unlock the notes, not even Microsoft Technical Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onenote/protect-notes-with-a-password-in-onenote-for-windows-10). Locked sections are left out of search until you unlock them.

The difference is scope. In Quillmark, encryption covers synced notes. In OneNote, you opt in section by section.

## Which works better offline?

Both work offline. Quillmark [works fully offline and sync resumes when you reconnect](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

OneNote [keeps a local copy of a notebook for offline editing and merges changes to the cloud on reconnect without creating conflicts](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onenote/onenote-help-and-learning/sync-a-notebook-in-onenote). Its notebooks are stored in OneDrive or SharePoint, and [the free edition only stores notebooks on OneDrive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_OneNote). Local notebooks are limited to the desktop app.

## Outlines, backlinks and search

Quillmark is built around outlines. [Every note is a tree you can fold, drag and zoom into](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/), and [one shortcut turns a bullet into its own note](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/features). Backlinks appear automatically when you type [[ to link a note. Semantic search finds a note by meaning, for example "that idea about onboarding emails", even when none of the words match.

OneNote takes the opposite approach. It [organizes pages into sections within notebooks, with no enforced page layout, and pages can be arbitrarily large](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_OneNote). Its search reaches into pictures, ink and audio. That suits clipping, annotating and sketching more than structured thinking.

## Publishing and sharing

Quillmark can [publish any note as a public page with a custom domain on Pro](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/features), and Teams adds shared spaces. OneNote is built for collaboration: its [Microsoft page covers sharing and collaboration](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/onenote/digital-note-taking-app) as well as Copilot summaries.

## Where OneNote is stronger

- **Price at zero:** [free on Mac, PC, iPhone, iPad and Android](https://www.businessinsider.com/reference/is-onenote-free), with no device limit mentioned.
- **Handwriting and drawing:** Microsoft lists [sketch, annotate and highlight, plus voice transcription](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/onenote/digital-note-taking-app).
- **AI:** Copilot in OneNote, [included with Microsoft 365 plans](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/onenote/digital-note-taking-app).
- **Web version:** Microsoft lists [Web among its sync platforms](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onenote/onenote-help-and-learning/sync-a-notebook-in-onenote). Quillmark's listed platforms are macOS, Windows, iOS and Android.

## Who should pick which

**Pick Quillmark if** you outline, link ideas and search by meaning, and want end-to-end encryption by default. It also suits students, who get Pro free, and writers who want to publish notes as pages.

**Pick OneNote if** you handwrite or sketch, already live in Microsoft 365, want AI summaries, or need a free app with no device cap.

**Use both if** you keep structured thinking in Quillmark and clippings or handwritten pages in OneNote. Quillmark exports Markdown and OPML on every plan.

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark a good OneNote alternative for students?**
Yes, if you work in outlines. Students get [Pro free with a school email](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing), including unlimited devices, version history, semantic search and publishing.

**Is OneNote end-to-end encrypted?**
Not for notebooks in general. [Microsoft holds the keys](https://scrib.blog/blog/is-onenote-safe) except for password-protected sections.

**Can I use Quillmark without internet?**
Yes. [It works fully offline and syncs when you reconnect](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

**Can I move my notes out of Quillmark?**
Yes. [Markdown and OPML export are on every plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

**Does OneNote cost money?**
The app is free for basic note taking. [Microsoft 365 Personal starts at $9.99 a month](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/onenote/digital-note-taking-app) if you want the bundle.

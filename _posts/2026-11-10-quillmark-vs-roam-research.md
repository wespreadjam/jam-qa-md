Last updated: November 2026

**Quillmark is the cheaper, more private option for people who outline offline; Roam Research is the stronger pick if you want a graph database with unlimited collaborators and an API.** Quillmark has a free plan, Pro at $8 per month and Pro free for students. Roam has no free plan and Pro costs [$15 per month](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing). Quillmark syncs end-to-end encrypted notes offline on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, while Roam is [cloud-hosted and not designed for extended offline use](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/roam-research-vs-logseq).

## Which costs less, Quillmark or Roam Research?

Quillmark. A solo user pays $0 on the Free plan or $8 per month on Pro. On Roam, Pro is [$15 per month](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing), almost double Quillmark Pro.

Roam's pricing page also shows [$165 per year](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing) and a $13.75 per month figure, without saying which billing option each applies to. Roam's Believer plan is [$500 up front for 5 years](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing), about $8.33 per month, and it adds first access to new features, community calls and priority support. It matches Quillmark Pro's monthly price only if you commit for five years.

Students get Quillmark Pro free. Roam's pricing page lists no student plan, although [Fabric reports a Scholar plan at $165 per year for .edu addresses](https://fabric.so/comparison/obsidian-vs-roam-research). Those two sources disagree, so confirm on Roam's checkout before relying on it.

For teams, Quillmark Teams is $14 per user per month. Roam Pro includes [unlimited collaborators](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing), so a large group sharing one graph can cost less on Roam than on a per-seat plan.

## Which works better offline and keeps notes private?

Quillmark. Sync is end-to-end encrypted and works offline on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android.

Roam stores graphs on its servers. [Speakwise describes it as cloud-hosted and not designed for extended offline use](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/roam-research-vs-logseq), and [Fabric lists it as cloud-only with no local files](https://fabric.so/comparison/obsidian-vs-roam-research). Roam does offer end-to-end encryption: a [third-party series](https://www.goedel.io/p/the-busy-architects-guide-to-roam) covers the feature Roam released in 2022. If you write on planes, trains or in libraries, offline-first sync matters more than an optional encryption setting.

## How do the outliners and backlinks differ?

Both are outliner-first. Quillmark gives you foldable outlines, automatic backlinks and semantic search, so you can find a note by meaning and not only by keyword.

Roam treats [every line as a block](https://fabric.so/comparison/obsidian-vs-roam-research). Each block can be referenced and embedded, daily notes are the default entry point, and a native query language builds database-style views. Roam also [can slow down with large graphs](https://fabric.so/comparison/obsidian-vs-roam-research), according to Fabric. If you want to query your notes like a database, Roam goes further. If you want to fold, link and search, Quillmark covers it at lower cost.

## What about collaboration, API access and publishing?

Roam has [real-time multiplayer editing with live cursors](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/roam-research-vs-logseq) and [API access on Pro](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing). If you build scripts or integrations on top of your notes, that is a real advantage for Roam.

Quillmark lets you publish notes as pages. Roam Pro supports [public graphs](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing). Both cover sharing writing on the web.

## Who should pick which?

Pick Quillmark if you:

- are a student, since Pro is free
- work offline and want encrypted sync across macOS, Windows, iOS and Android
- outline long drafts and want foldable structure, backlinks and semantic search
- want a free tier before paying

Pick Roam Research if you:

- want block references, native queries and a graph database model
- need API access or a large group editing one graph live
- already have years of notes in Roam
- are willing to pay [$15 per month or commit to Believer](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing)

## FAQ

**Is Roam Research free?** No. It has [no free plan and a 31-day trial](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/roam-research-vs-logseq). Quillmark has a Free plan.

**Does Roam Research work offline?** Third-party reviews describe it as [cloud-dependent and not designed for extended offline use](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/roam-research-vs-logseq). Quillmark syncs offline with end-to-end encryption.

**Can I move my notes from Roam to another app?** Roam [exports to JSON and Markdown](https://fabric.so/comparison/obsidian-vs-roam-research). Block references do not always convert cleanly in other tools.

**Which has a student discount?** Quillmark gives students Pro free. Roam's [pricing page](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing) shows none.

**Which is better for teams?** Roam Pro includes [unlimited collaborators](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing). Quillmark Teams is $14 per user per month.

## Next steps

Start on Quillmark's Free plan and import a few outlines to test folding, backlinks and semantic search. Students can switch to Pro at no cost. If you need Roam's API or live multi-user editing, start Roam's [31-day trial](https://roamresearch.com/#/app/ro-pricing) with a real project before you buy Believer.

Last updated: November 2026

Quillmark and Workflowy are both outliner-first note apps. Pick Quillmark if you want end-to-end encrypted offline sync, semantic search and notes you can publish as pages. Pick Workflowy if you want the lower annual price, Linux apps and mirrors. Both are free to start, and both give students free Pro.

## How much do Quillmark and Workflowy cost?

Quillmark has a free plan, Pro at $8 per month and Teams at $14 per user per month. Students get Pro free.

Workflowy's Basic plan is free forever with [100 nodes per month and 100mb file uploads](https://workflowy.com/pricing/). Pro costs $6.99 per month billed annually or $8.99 per month, and includes unlimited nodes and uploads. In the iOS app, [Pro is listed at $8.99 monthly or $79.99 yearly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/workflowy-note-list-outline/id551139514).

On the Workflowy free plan, [you can still add nodes after the limit, but a popup invites you to subscribe](https://workflowy.com/pricing/). Workflowy Teams is [$6.99 per user per month, paid yearly](https://workflowy.com/teams), and adds one-on-one team setup and auto-backup to Dropbox. That is about half the Quillmark Teams price per seat.

Discounts differ. Workflowy gives [free Pro for life to university faculty and for the length of a course to students, and 50% off Pro for non-profits](https://workflowy.com/pricing/). Quillmark gives students Pro free.

## Which is more private: Quillmark or Workflowy?

Quillmark uses end-to-end encrypted sync, which means only your devices can read your notes. It works offline.

A reply in Workflowy's community forum says [Workflowy does not have end-to-end encryption](https://community.workflowy.com/t/privacy-security/385), meaning the company itself can decrypt your data. The same thread points to a third-party Chrome extension for client-side encryption that, per the thread, does not yet encrypt files. Workflowy does work offline: [its apps let you view and edit outlines offline and sync when you reconnect](https://research.com/software/reviews/workflowy).

If you handle interviews, unpublished research or client material, encryption is the deciding difference.

## Backlinks, mirrors and search

Quillmark creates backlinks automatically, so linking a note shows the connection on both sides.

Workflowy links are explicit. You create an internal link with [the "[[" shortcut, the bullet menu or a slash command, and a Backlink item under the linked bullet lists every place that links to it](https://community.workflowy.com/t/links-and-backlinks/1676). Workflowy also has mirrors, which [show the same bullet in several places and keep every copy in sync](https://workflowy.com/help/mirrors). That is useful for a task that belongs to two projects.

For search, Quillmark offers semantic search, which finds notes by meaning. Workflowy offers [real-time search across the outline](https://research.com/software/reviews/workflowy).

## Platforms

Quillmark runs on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android. Workflowy covers those and adds [Linux packages (AppImage, .deb and .rpm) and Chrome and Firefox clipper extensions](https://workflowy.com/download). If you use Linux, Workflowy is the option today.

## Who should pick which

Choose Quillmark if:
- You need notes no one but you can decrypt.
- You want backlinks without setting them up.
- You want to publish notes as pages.
- You are a student, researcher or writer who wants semantic search across a large archive.

Choose Workflowy if:
- You want the lowest annual price: [$6.99 per month for Pro](https://workflowy.com/pricing/) or [per team seat](https://workflowy.com/teams).
- You use Linux.
- You rely on mirrors to keep one task in several lists.
- You want browser clippers for Chrome or Firefox.

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark a good Workflowy alternative?**
Yes, if you want an outliner with foldable outlines, automatic backlinks, semantic search and end-to-end encrypted offline sync.

**Is Workflowy free?**
Yes. The Basic plan is [free forever with 100 nodes per month](https://workflowy.com/pricing/). Pro removes the limit.

**Does Workflowy have end-to-end encryption?**
A reply in Workflowy's community forum says [it does not](https://community.workflowy.com/t/privacy-security/385). Quillmark does.

**What happens to my Workflowy data if I stop paying for Pro?**
Workflowy says [you keep all of your data and can access it forever](https://workflowy.com/pricing/).

**Which is cheaper for a team?**
[Workflowy Teams is $6.99 per user per month paid yearly](https://workflowy.com/teams). Quillmark Teams is $14 per user per month.

**Can I use both apps on my phone and laptop?**
Yes. Quillmark runs on iOS, Android, macOS and Windows. Workflowy has [iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux apps](https://workflowy.com/download).

## Next steps

Try both on the same outline. Import a real project into the free plan of each, work offline for a day, and see which linking and search style suits you. If you are a student, start with Quillmark Pro, which is free.

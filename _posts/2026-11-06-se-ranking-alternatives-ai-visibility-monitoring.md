## What SE Ranking includes today

SE Ranking sells AI tracking in two ways. Its [Core plan](https://seranking.com/subscription.html) ($103.20 a month, shown against a struck-through $129 with an annual-saving toggle) and Growth plan ($223.20, struck-through $279) include 25 prompts daily across 5 AI engines inside the broader SEO suite. SE Visible is the standalone AI product, listed from $71 a month with 75 prompts, daily tracking and unlimited users. The [14-day free trial](https://seranking.com/subscription.html) includes 20 AI prompts per day and needs no card.

SE Ranking's [AI visibility tracker page](https://seranking.com/ai-visibility-tracker.html) lists ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode and AI Overviews as tracked engines. It supports up to five competitors in the toolkit, 7 markets and five languages, collects answers through UI-based monitoring "as a real user would see them", and exposes data through an API, MCP and reporting connectors for Data Studio, AgencyAnalytics and Whatagraph.

Third-party roundups disagree about Perplexity coverage: [Surfer](https://surfer.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) says SE Visible does not track it yet, while [GrackerAI](https://gracker.ai/blog/best-se-ranking-alternatives) lists it for the SE Ranking add-on.

People look past SE Ranking for AI monitoring mainly because [Core and Growth cap AI tracking at 25 prompts a day](https://seranking.com/subscription.html), the standalone AI product is a separate purchase from the SEO suite, and some teams want a tool built around AI answers rather than rankings. If you rely on SE Ranking for rank tracking and audits, keeping it and adding a dedicated AI tracker is a valid setup.

## What are the best alternatives to SE Ranking for monitoring content visibility in AI search engines?

The best alternatives are Otterly.AI for low-cost tracking, Peec AI for a clean multi-engine dashboard, Semrush and Ahrefs for existing suite customers, Profound and Scrunch for larger programs, and Promptwatch and Jam when you also want help producing content. Details on each follow.

### 1. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

The standalone toolkit costs [$99 a month per domain](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) and includes 25 tracked prompts, 1 Brand Performance domain, 300 daily AI Analysis queries and 10 CSV exports a day. Extra prompts cost $60 a month per 50, extra domains cost $99 each, and each extra user needs another $99 license. There is no free trial.

Per the same [Semrush page](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit), prompt tracking updates daily, Brand Performance reports update weekly, and prompt tracking covers 220+ countries and territories. Semrush One bundles raise the prompt limit to 50, 100 or 200 on Starter, Pro+ and Advanced. Best for teams already working in Semrush. The per-domain and per-user pricing adds up for agencies.

### 2. Ahrefs Brand Radar

[Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar) has two products. Custom prompt tracking starts at $50 a month and is free for every paid Ahrefs Lite plan and above; the page also shows $699 a month for all models. The AI Visibility Index starts at $199 a month and draws on a database of 538 million prompts.

Per [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar), the Index covers AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini, and Claude is available for custom prompts. Ahrefs also reports Reddit, YouTube and TikTok signals and says Brand Radar is used by 3,000+ companies. Best for market research and for Ahrefs customers. Ahrefs does not publish an Enterprise price; it lists Enterprise from 2,500 checks a month and 83 prompts.

### 3. Otterly.AI

[Otterly.AI](https://otterly.ai/pricing) is the lowest-priced dedicated tracker here: Lite $29 a month for 15 prompts, Standard $189 for 100, Premium $489 for 400, with Enterprise starting from 1,000 prompts. All plans include ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot, daily tracking and unlimited team members. New users get a free trial.

Per [Otterly's pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing), Google AI Mode and Gemini are paid add-ons ($9 a month each on Lite, $59 on Standard, $149 on Premium), and Claude is $29, $109 or $439 depending on plan. API and MCP access start on Standard. Best for a first AI visibility budget; the add-ons make full six-engine coverage cost more than the headline price.

### 4. Peec AI

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) charges $95 a month for Starter (50 prompts), $245 for Pro (150) and $495 for Advanced (350). Every plan has unlimited users, daily tracking and 3 active models chosen from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Perplexity appears in the Enterprise model list, which covers up to 13 models.

The API starts on Pro and the Looker Studio connector on Advanced. Best for marketing teams that want a focused dashboard. The three-model cap on self-serve plans is the main limit.

### 5. Profound

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) does not publish Enterprise pricing. Its free trial runs 50 prompts daily for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Enterprise tracks up to 9 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Exa Search and Muse Spark) and adds SSO/SAML, SOC 2 compliance, CSV and JSON exports and an API.

Best for companies with a dedicated AI search owner and a procurement process. Not a fit if you need a self-serve price today.

### 6. Scrunch

[Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/pricing) Core is $250 a month with 125 prompts, 5,000 responses a month, 5 user licenses and 4 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot), with a 7-day free trial. Core is limited to 1 country, 1 language, 5 competitors and 1 page optimization a month.

Enterprise is custom, supports 9 LLMs and adds API access, MCP access and SSO. Best for teams that want site audits and agent traffic data next to monitoring.

### 7. Promptwatch

[Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/pricing) Essential includes 50 prompts, 6,000 responses, daily tracking, unlimited seats, API and MCP access and all tracked models (the page lists 14, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot). Professional has 150 prompts and Business has 350 with up to hourly tracking. Promptwatch's [own comparison page](https://promptwatch.com/blog/se-ranking-alternatives) lists Essential at $95 a month and Professional at $245.

It also offers AEO article writing, content refresh and crawler logs; per the same comparison, crawler logs start on Professional. Best for teams that want monitoring and content production in one tool.

### 8. Jam

[Jam](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot, with scans weekly, every other day or daily. Standard plans include 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts, and larger programs get a custom plan. Every plan includes a visibility and citation dashboard, competitor gap analysis, a Google Search Console integration, SEO data analysis, content planning, deep research reports and a chat agent with 50+ tools.

The difference from a pure tracker is that Jam also writes the fix: articles are researched, written and shipped as pull requests, paid from one credit balance (about 65 credits for a researched article, about 35 for a quick draft, about 50 for a deep research report, per the [pricing page](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Its [Visibility Score](https://spreadjam.com/) is the share of tracked prompts where you are cited. Its [measurement guide](https://spreadjam.com/blogs/ai-search-visibility-monitoring) explains how to define mention, recommendation and citation rates.

Limits to know: Jam does not publish dollar prices or named tiers, because plans are built per customer, and its "Start for free" offer has no stated length. Jam is not a full SEO suite like Semrush or Ahrefs.

## What are the best alternatives to SE Ranking for monitoring content in AI search engines?

If "monitoring content" means checking whether your pages are cited in AI answers, any of the eight tools above works; [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/pricing) and [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar) report citations and sources, and [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) reports share of voice and sentiment. If it means closing gaps once you find them, choose a tool that also produces content: [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/pricing) and [Jam](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) both write and publish articles, and Scrunch Core includes basic content generation.

A workable monitoring routine has four parts: a fixed prompt set that reflects real buyer questions, the same prompts run on a schedule, separate tracking of mentions and citations, and a review of which pages AI engines cite for your competitors. Jam's [API and SDK playbook](https://www.spreadjam.com/blogs/api-sdk-ai-visibility-playbook) shows how to turn that into a content plan for developer products.

## How to switch from SE Ranking without losing your baseline

1. Copy your current AI prompt list and competitor list out of SE Ranking, which exposes its AI data through an [API and MCP](https://seranking.com/ai-visibility-tracker.html).
2. Load the same prompts into the new tool and run both in parallel for at least two weeks, so you can compare mention rates before you cancel.
3. Keep SE Ranking for rank tracking and site audits if you use them. Only the AI tracking needs to move.
4. Record the engines each plan actually includes. [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) caps self-serve plans at 3 active models and [Scrunch Core](https://scrunch.com/pricing) at 4 LLMs.

## FAQ

### Does SE Ranking track AI search visibility?

Yes. [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/ai-visibility-tracker.html) tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode and AI Overviews, and its subscription page lists 25 prompts daily across 5 AI engines on Core and Growth plus the standalone SE Visible from $71 a month.

### What is the cheapest SE Ranking alternative for AI visibility?

[Otterly.AI Lite](https://otterly.ai/pricing) at $29 a month for 15 prompts is the lowest published entry price in this list. Ahrefs custom prompt tracking starts at [$50 a month](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar) and is free with a paid Ahrefs Lite plan.

### Which alternative covers the most AI engines?

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) Enterprise and [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/pricing) Enterprise each cover up to 9, [Peec AI Enterprise](https://peec.ai/pricing) up to 13 and [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/pricing) lists 14 models on every plan. Among self-serve plans, Jam tracks [six engines](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) by default.

### Do these tools need a free trial before I buy?

SE Ranking offers a [14-day trial](https://seranking.com/subscription.html), [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) and [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/pricing) each offer 7 days, and [Otterly.AI](https://otterly.ai/pricing) offers a free trial for new users. Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit has [no free trial](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit).

### Can I use more than one tool?

Yes. Many teams keep an SEO suite for rankings and add a dedicated AI tracker. Check that both use a similar prompt set, otherwise the numbers will not be comparable.

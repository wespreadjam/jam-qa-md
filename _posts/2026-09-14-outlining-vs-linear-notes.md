title: Outlining vs linear notes, and when each wins
description: Why outline-first notes beat long documents for research, and where they fall short.
---

Linear documents are great for finished writing. Outlines are better for thinking, because they let you
move ideas without rewriting them. In a study of 200 Quillmark users, people who outlined first
finished long-form drafts 30% faster than those who wrote top to bottom.

## When to outline
- Literature reviews and research notes
- Meeting notes you will revisit
- Planning a project with many moving parts

## When to write linearly
- Essays where voice and flow matter more than structure
- Emails and short updates

Last updated: November 2026

**Pick Quillmark if you think in outlines and want notes that only you can read. Pick Craft if you want block-based documents, real-time collaboration and an AI assistant.** Quillmark [encrypts notes end to end and works fully offline](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/). Craft [does not offer end-to-end encryption](https://support.craft.do/en/account-and-subscription/data-and-security/encryption) because its sync and collaboration need server-side access. Both have a free plan, and both give students a free paid tier.

## How much do Quillmark and Craft cost?

Quillmark has three plans: [Free at $0, Pro at $8/month and Teams at $14/user/month](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing). Every paid plan has a 14-day refund window. Free limits you to 2 devices, while Pro removes the device cap and adds version history, semantic search and publishing.

Craft's own pages disagree on price. Its [pricing page](https://www.craft.do/pricing) shows Plus at $6.4/month (with $8.0 struck through), Family at $12/month and Team at $5/user/month. Its [help center](https://support.craft.do/en/account-and-subscription/subscription-plans/plans-and-pricing) lists Plus at $96/year or $10/month, Family at $180/year or $18/month, and Team at $60/member/year or $6/member/month. Craft also says prices vary by country. Check the price for your region at checkout.

The free plans limit different things. Quillmark limits devices (2). Craft limits content: [1,500 blocks, 1 GB of storage, a 25 MB media upload limit and 7 days of version history](https://www.craft.do/pricing).

## Is Craft end-to-end encrypted?

No. Craft states it [does not currently offer end-to-end encryption](https://support.craft.do/en/account-and-subscription/data-and-security/encryption), and explains that real-time sync, collaboration and multi-device access need server-side data handling. It encrypts data in transit with TLS, encrypts documents at rest with AWS RDS encryption and files with SSE-S3, and reports SOC 2 Type I and II certification.

Quillmark notes are [end-to-end encrypted](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/). If you keep sensitive research, interviews or drafts in your notes, that is the main difference between the two apps. The trade-off is that a server-side design makes live collaboration simpler, which is the reason Craft gives.

## Which works better offline?

Both work offline. Quillmark [works fully offline and resumes sync when you reconnect](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

Craft supports offline on every platform, but setup differs. On [Mac, iPhone and iPad it works out of the box; on Web, Windows and Android you turn on Offline Mode, open the document once while online, and closed documents sync only the next time you open them](https://support.craft.do/en/introduction/offline). Craft's [platform page](https://support.craft.do/en/introduction/platforms) adds that the Android app launched in November 2025 and is still being developed.

## Which is better for outlining?

Quillmark is built around the outline: [every note is a tree you can fold, drag and zoom into](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/), and backlinks appear when you mention a note.

Craft is a block editor. It has [toggles that collapse and expand nested content](https://support.craft.do/en/write-and-edit/blocks-and-pages/toggles), and backlinks created automatically when you [@-mention a document or block](https://support.craft.do/en/organize-and-find/linking), shown under "Links To This Page". Craft links work only inside the same Space. You can build an outline in Craft, but a toggle is a feature of a document, not the structure of the whole note.

## Publishing and export

Quillmark lists publishing on the [Pro plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) and Markdown and OPML export on every plan. OPML matters if you move outlines between outliner apps.

Craft lets you publish on every plan. Its [pricing page](https://www.craft.do/pricing) lists unlimited published pages, plus password protection, expiry dates, hiding the "Created with Craft" badge and search indexing on Plus and above. Free includes 1-click publish.

## Do students get a free plan?

Both do. Quillmark gives students [Pro free with a school email, the same Pro plan that costs $8/month for everyone else](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing). You keep it until the school email expires.

Craft's [Education plan](https://www.craft.do/pricing) is free, gives you everything Plus offers, and needs school-email verification once a year. Craft also offers up to 50% off Plus and Team for charities, schools and educators.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Quillmark if:**
- You write in outlines and want to fold, drag and zoom into every note.
- You want end-to-end encryption and full offline use on every platform.
- You want Markdown and OPML export on the free plan.

**Pick Craft if:**
- You want rich, designed documents built from blocks, with real-time collaboration.
- You need an AI assistant, a web app, or a client for Vision Pro.
- You want a low per-seat team price. Craft Team is [$5/user/month](https://www.craft.do/pricing) against Quillmark Teams at [$14](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing), though Quillmark Teams includes SSO and Craft only offers SSO on Enterprise.
- You need unlimited free publishing with custom branding.

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark a Craft alternative?**
Yes, for people who mainly outline and care about privacy. It does not list an AI assistant or a web app, which Craft has.

**Does Craft have a free plan?**
Yes. It includes [1,500 blocks, 1 GB of storage and 7 days of version history](https://www.craft.do/pricing).

**Can I export my notes from Quillmark?**
Yes, [Markdown and OPML export are on every plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

**Which platforms does each app run on?**
Quillmark runs on [macOS, Windows, iOS and Android](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/). Craft runs on [macOS, iOS, Windows (x64 and ARM64), Android and the web](https://support.craft.do/en/introduction/platforms).

## Next steps

Try the [Quillmark Free plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) on two devices with one real project. If you are a student, sign up with your school email to get Pro. If you want to compare against Craft, move one project into each app and judge which structure helps you think.

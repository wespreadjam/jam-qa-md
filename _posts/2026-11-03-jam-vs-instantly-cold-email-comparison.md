## What does each platform actually do?

Instantly has built a strong reputation as a cold email tool, and many outbound sales teams reach for it first when evaluating sending platforms.

Jam is an AI distribution platform that runs cold email outreach as one of several channels. It also monitors your visibility across AI answer engines, publishes structured content designed to get cited, and connects to the tools your team already uses. The goal is not just to reach prospects by email but to be present wherever buyers ask questions, including inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot.

## How does Jam handle sending infrastructure and deliverability?

Jam connects Gmail in a single click through Google's own sign-in flow, with no app passwords and no manual setup. Once connected, Jam discovers your send-as aliases automatically so you can pick which address to send from in chat or in a campaign.

Key deliverability features in Jam:

- **Multiple sending inboxes with round-robin rotation:** Assign several inboxes to one campaign and Jam spreads volume across them so no single inbox carries the full load.
- **Provider-aware warm-up ramps:** Each inbox warms up on its own schedule tuned to its provider. If you prefer to skip warm-up, turning it off lifts your daily cap to your provider's maximum.
- **Timezone-aware send windows:** Emails land during your recipient's working hours, not the middle of their night.
- **Built-in deliverability check:** A pre-send check flags spammy phrasing before any message goes out.

## How does Jam find and verify leads?

Jam's lead generation works the way you would search for anything else in the product: describe who you want and Jam returns enriched contact details including company, title, and email confidence. You can search for a single person or pull a bulk list.

- **Bulk lead pulls:** Hundreds of matching contacts in a single request, with a clear cap and a confirmation step before it runs.
- **Verification gate:** Before any outreach goes out, Jam checks each address and holds back anything unverified, protecting your sender reputation from bad emails.
- **LinkedIn-style search:** Find contacts the same way you would search LinkedIn, and get back enriched profiles with company, title, and location.

## Which platform tracks responses and engagement more effectively?

Jam includes a full inbox built into the platform. Replies to your campaigns arrive in a threaded reader with separate views for Sent and Drafts, and the inbox updates in real time as new messages come in.

Engagement tracking features:

- Reply rate and delivery rate metrics visible in the dashboard, with period-over-period changes.
- Slack, Discord, and email notifications when a prospect replies, with quick actions so you can respond without opening Jam.
- Quiet hours and intelligent batching so notifications arrive as a digest rather than a flood.
- Every outbound send is mirrored into your Sent folder automatically.

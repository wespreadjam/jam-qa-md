Last updated: November 2026

Pick Quillmark if you think in outlines and want notes that are [end-to-end encrypted and work fully offline](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/). Pick Evernote if your main job is capturing and searching web pages, PDFs and images, or if you need its calendar, task and Slack and Google integrations. Quillmark Pro costs [$8 a month and is free for students](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing); Evernote Starter costs [$99 a year](https://evernote.com/pricing) and its free plan is capped at 50 notes.

## How do Quillmark and Evernote pricing compare?

Both have a free plan, but the limits differ. Quillmark Free has [unlimited notes on 2 devices](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing). Evernote Free allows [50 notes, 1 notebook and 1 device, with 1 GB of monthly uploads](https://evernote.com/pricing).

At the entry paid tier the prices are close. Quillmark Pro is [$8 a month](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) and includes unlimited devices. Evernote Starter is [$8.25 a month or $99 a year](https://evernote.com/pricing) but is limited to [1000 notes, 20 notebooks and 3 devices](https://evernote.com/compare-plans). To get unlimited notes and devices on Evernote you move to Advanced at [$249.99 a year](https://evernote.com/pricing), which carries [safeguard limits against abuse](https://evernote.com/pricing).

Quillmark has [a 14-day refund window](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) on every paid plan. Evernote's [paid-plan trials last 7 days by default](https://evernote.com/pricing), and you are not charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

For teams, Quillmark Teams is [$14 per user a month](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) with shared spaces, admin controls, SSO and priority support. Evernote Flexible starts [from $10 per seat a month](https://evernote.com/pricing). Evernote Enterprise is for [10 or more seats](https://evernote.com/pricing) and adds SSO, SCIM and a dedicated Customer Success Manager. Evernote does not publish Enterprise pricing on its [pricing page](https://evernote.com/pricing).

## Which app is more private?

Quillmark notes are [end-to-end encrypted](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/). Evernote's [security overview](https://evernote.com/security) describes TLS for data in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest using Google-managed keys, and it does not describe end-to-end encryption. Evernote does list [in-note encryption on Mac and Windows](https://evernote.com/compare-plans), which protects selected text rather than the whole account.

Evernote's security program is broader than Quillmark's published detail. It is [ISO 27001 certified as of November 2025](https://evernote.com/security), runs a public bug bounty, and offers [two-factor authentication](https://evernote.com/security). Evernote data [resides inside the United States](https://evernote.com/security). Quillmark's site does not explain key handling or publish a certification.

## Which app works better offline?

Quillmark supports offline use [fully, and sync resumes when you reconnect](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing). Evernote lists [offline notes](https://evernote.com/compare-plans) among its features. Its plan page does not show which plans include it, so confirm that on your plan before you rely on it.

## Which is better for outlining and linking notes?

Quillmark is built around outlines: [every note is a tree you can fold, drag and zoom into](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/), and mentioning a note creates a [link in both directions](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/) without setup. Outlines and backlinks are on the [Free plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

Evernote organizes with notebooks, spaces and tags, with [limits per plan](https://evernote.com/compare-plans). One roundup describes its [pages as linear](https://www.atlasworkspace.ai/blog/alternatives-to-evernote), so it suits capture and filing better than nested thinking.

## How do search and AI features compare?

Quillmark's search finds notes [by meaning, not by the exact word you typed](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/), and semantic search is part of [Pro](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing).

Evernote has a wider AI set: [AI Assistant, Semantic Search, AI Transcribe, AI Edit, AI Cleanup (mobile only) and an MCP connection](https://evernote.com/compare-plans) to Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP tools. It also offers [Boolean, geographic and filtered search](https://evernote.com/compare-plans), and a roundup notes it [can search images, PDFs and handwriting](https://www.atlasworkspace.ai/blog/alternatives-to-evernote). Quillmark does not publish equivalents for AI transcription, OCR or web clipping.

## Can I export my notes and switch?

Quillmark offers [Markdown and OPML export on every plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing). Evernote lets you [export notes and notebooks as PDFs](https://evernote.com/compare-plans) and uses [ENEX, a documented XML export](https://www.atlasworkspace.ai/blog/alternatives-to-evernote) that keeps notes, files, tags and creation dates. If you move from Evernote, the same roundup suggests keeping Evernote active for 30 days as a [read-only fallback](https://www.atlasworkspace.ai/blog/alternatives-to-evernote) and testing with a small notebook first.

## Is there a student discount?

Quillmark gives students [Pro free with a school email](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) and they keep it until the school email expires. Evernote gives eligible college students [40% off a one-year Advanced subscription](https://evernote.com/pricing), which is a discount rather than a free plan.

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Quillmark if you:**
- write, research or study in outlines and want foldable notes with automatic backlinks
- want end-to-end encryption and full offline use
- are a student and want Pro at no cost
- want unlimited notes and devices at $8 a month

**Pick Evernote if you:**
- clip web pages, PDFs and images and need to search inside them
- need calendar, tasks, Google, Outlook or Slack integrations
- want AI transcription or an MCP connection to your AI tools
- need Enterprise controls such as SCIM and a dedicated Customer Success Manager

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark a good Evernote alternative?**
Yes for outline-based work, private notes and offline use. It is not a substitute for Evernote's web clipping, OCR or integrations, which Quillmark does not publish.

**Is Evernote end-to-end encrypted?**
Evernote's [security overview](https://evernote.com/security) describes encryption in transit and at rest with Google-managed keys and does not describe end-to-end encryption. Quillmark notes are [end-to-end encrypted](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/).

**Is Quillmark free?**
Yes. The [Free plan](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) has unlimited notes on 2 devices, outlines and backlinks. Pro is $8 a month, and students get it free.

**How many notes does Evernote Free allow?**
[50 notes and 1 notebook](https://evernote.com/pricing), on 1 device.

**Can I take my notes with me if I leave Quillmark?**
Yes. [Markdown and OPML export](https://wespreadjam.github.io/jam-qa-md/pricing) are on every plan.

Last updated: November 2026

**Short answer:** Pick Quillmark if you take lecture notes as outlines and want backlinks, encrypted offline sync and a free Pro plan without setup. Pick Obsidian if you want free local Markdown files, a large plugin library and Linux support, and you are happy to configure it yourself. Obsidian's app is [free for personal and educational use](https://obsidian.md/license), but its [Sync add-on costs extra](https://obsidian.md/pricing) and Quillmark's Pro plan is free for students.

## Which is better for lecture notes: outlining and backlinks

Quillmark is built around the outline. Every note is a foldable outline, so you can nest a lecture as topic, point and example, then fold it for review. Backlinks are automatic, so linking a concept in week 3 to the same concept in week 9 shows up on both notes. Semantic search finds notes by meaning rather than exact words.

Obsidian is a Markdown document app first. Its [Outline plugin lists the headings of the active note and lets you drag a heading to rearrange sections](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins/outline). Folding works on headings and lists, but [users request separate fold controls for headings and lists](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/option-to-fold-unfold-headers-independently-of-lists/31727), and a community plugin is a common workaround. If you think in bullets, expect some setup.

On backlinks, Obsidian is strong. Its [Backlinks plugin shows linked mentions and unlinked mentions](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins/backlinks), can show the surrounding paragraph, filters results and can place backlinks at the bottom of a note. Obsidian also adds a graph view and a Canvas. Neither has built-in flashcards: [Obsidian has no first-party flashcard scheduler](https://brainden.app/blog/remnote-vs-obsidian), so if spaced repetition matters most, a dedicated study app such as RemNote may suit you better than either.

## Offline use and sync cost

Both apps work without a connection. Obsidian [stores notes on your device so you can open them even offline](https://obsidian.md/), and its [Sync merges changes when you reconnect](https://obsidian.md/sync). Quillmark uses end-to-end encrypted offline sync, so notes written on a plane or in a lecture hall without Wi-Fi reach your other devices later.

The difference is cost. Obsidian's local storage is free, but syncing across devices needs a paid add-on:

- [Sync Standard: $4 per month billed annually, or $5 billed monthly. 1 synced vault, 1 GB storage, 5 MB maximum file size, 1 month of version history.](https://obsidian.md/sync)
- [Sync Plus: $8 or $10 per month. 10 synced vaults, 10 GB storage, 200 MB maximum file size, 12 months of version history.](https://obsidian.md/sync)

Without Sync, you move your vault between devices yourself, for example through a folder service you already use. With Quillmark, encrypted sync is a built-in part of the app.

## Price for students

Obsidian's app costs nothing: it is [free for all purposes, including educational institutions, and needs no account](https://obsidian.md/license). What students may pay for is Sync or Publish, and Obsidian gives [students, faculty and nonprofit employees 40% off both](https://obsidian.md/pricing). The discount applies to the [$4 per month annual Sync Standard price](https://obsidian.md/sync). [Refunds are available within 7 days](https://obsidian.md/pricing).

Quillmark has a Free plan, Pro at $8 per month and Teams at $14 per user per month. Students get Pro free, so there is no subscription to budget for.

Obsidian does not publish how student status is verified on its [pricing page](https://obsidian.md/pricing).

## Who should pick which

**Pick Quillmark if you:**
- take notes as nested bullets and want to fold them for revision
- want backlinks without configuring anything
- study on macOS, Windows, iPhone or Android and want encrypted sync included
- want Pro features at no cost as a student

**Pick Obsidian if you:**
- want your notes as plain Markdown files in a local folder that [other text editors can read](https://brainden.app/blog/remnote-vs-obsidian)
- use Linux
- like customizing your setup with plugins and themes
- want a graph view or Canvas for visual brainstorming
- are fine moving files between devices yourself, or paying for Sync at a student discount

## FAQ

**Is Quillmark or Obsidian better for lecture notes?**
Quillmark fits outline-style lecture notes with little setup. Obsidian fits students who want local Markdown files and are willing to customize.

**Is Obsidian free for students?**
Yes. The app is [free for personal and educational use](https://obsidian.md/license). Sync and Publish are paid, with [40% off for students](https://obsidian.md/pricing).

**Does Obsidian have backlinks?**
Yes. The [Backlinks core plugin](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins/backlinks) shows linked and unlinked mentions for each note.

**Does Obsidian work offline?**
Yes. [Notes live on your device](https://obsidian.md/), so you can open them without a connection.

**Is Quillmark free for students?**
Yes. Students get the Pro plan free.

**Can I publish notes from either app?**
Yes. Quillmark publishes notes as pages. Obsidian offers [Publish at $8 per site per month billed annually](https://obsidian.md/pricing).

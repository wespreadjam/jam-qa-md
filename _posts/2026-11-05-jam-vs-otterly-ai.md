Last updated: November 2026

**Jam and Otterly.ai both track your brand in AI answers and show where competitors are cited and you are not. Otterly is a monitoring and audit tool with a low entry price ([$29 a month for 15 prompts](https://otterly.ai/pricing)). Jam includes all [six of its engines in one plan](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) and goes on to write the articles that close the gaps and ship them as pull requests. For a content marketer choosing a competitor gap analysis tool in 2026, the question is whether you want a dashboard that tells you what is missing or a workflow that also produces the fix.**

## What does each tool track?

**Jam** tracks six platforms: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. You choose how many prompts to track (50, 200, 400, 600, 800 or 1,000) and how often they are scanned. Results appear in a visibility and citation dashboard ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)). Jam's Visibility Score is the [share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://www.spreadjam.com/). Jam's pricing page does not list Claude among its platforms.

**Otterly.ai** runs four engines on every plan: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot. Claude, Google AI Mode and Gemini are sold as add-ons, which makes seven engines in total ([Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing), [SE Ranking](https://visible.seranking.com/blog/otterly-ai-review)). Upgrading a plan raises the prompt count, not the engine count. Beyond tracking, Otterly offers a GEO audit with a crawlability checker that tests about twenty named AI user agents, and a Brand Visibility Index that plots brand coverage against a "likelihood to buy" score ([SE Ranking](https://visible.seranking.com/blog/otterly-ai-review)).

## Which is better for competitor gap analysis?

Both tools answer the same first question: on which prompts do AI answers name your competitors and not you?

**Otterly's Content Gap Analyzer** filters the prompts where you are absent, shows which brands appear instead, lists the sources the engine used to name them, and splits the result by engine. It ranks the gaps and describes a hand-off into a work queue ([Otterly gap analysis](https://otterly.ai/features/ai-search-content-gap-analysis)). The page is candid that many gaps are a missing third-party mention rather than missing content on your own site, and that closing those needs an outreach or brand placement program.

**Jam's gap analysis** finds where you are not cited across the platforms it tracks, then carries on to the fix: a researched article shipped as a pull request, with approvals in Slack and GitHub ([Jam homepage](https://www.spreadjam.com/), [Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)). Jam also has the outreach side. It connects mailboxes, enriches leads, warms up mailboxes and triages replies, which is the tool for the third-party placement work Otterly's own page says you will need ([Jam homepage](https://www.spreadjam.com/)).

Otterly's strength is depth of analysis and reporting: Brand Visibility Index, domain citation categories, query fan-out and GEO audits ([SE Ranking](https://visible.seranking.com/blog/otterly-ai-review)). Jam's strength is that the gap list and the work to close it sit in one product with one credit balance.

## Who should pick Jam, and who should pick Otterly.ai?

**Pick Otterly.ai if:**
- You want the cheapest way to start. Lite is $29 a month for 15 prompts, with unlimited team members ([Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing)).
- Your engines are ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot, and daily tracking matters.
- You need Claude coverage, API or MCP access, or a Looker Studio connector. Otterly lists all of these; Jam's pricing page does not.
- You run an agency. Otterly has an agency program with unlimited workspaces and extra prompts ([Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing)).
- You already have writers and an outreach process, and need a tracking and audit layer.

**Pick Jam if:**
- You need Gemini and Google AI Mode tracked without paying add-on fees.
- You track 200 to 1,000 prompts and want one plan rather than stacking prompt packs and engine add-ons.
- You want the gaps turned into drafted articles and pull requests rather than a task list.
- You want outreach to third-party sites in the same product as tracking.
- You are a small growth team or founder who would rather approve work than do it.

## Where is Jam behind Otterly.ai?

- **Published prices.** Jam's pricing page text shows one price, $299 for 400 prompts. Otterly shows every tier and add-on.
- **Entry cost.** Otterly's $29 Lite plan is the cheaper way to try prompt tracking at small volume.
- **Engines.** Otterly offers Claude as an add-on. Jam lists six platforms and not Claude.
- **Scan frequency at 400 prompts.** Jam's default setting scans every other day. Otterly lists daily tracking on all plans.
- **Reporting integrations.** Otterly lists a Data Studio connector, API and MCP access. Jam's pricing page does not list them.
- **Track record.** Otterly launched in October 2024 and reports more than 40,000 users, and SE Ranking notes a 2025 Gartner Cool Vendor listing ([SE Ranking](https://visible.seranking.com/blog/otterly-ai-review)). Those are company-reported figures.

## FAQ

**How much does Otterly.ai cost compared with Jam?**
Otterly is $29, $189 or $489 a month for 15, 100 or 400 prompts on four engines, with add-ons for Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude ([Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing)). Jam is $299 a month at its default 400 prompt setting across six platforms, including 2,000 work credits ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)).

**Which AI engines does each track?**
Jam: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. Otterly: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot by default, plus Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude as add-ons.

**Does Otterly.ai do more than monitoring?**
Yes. It includes GEO audits, prompt research and optimization recommendations, and Jam's own buying guide notes that Otterly [describes content audits and briefs as well as monitoring](https://www.spreadjam.com/blogs/best-ai-visibility-tools). Jam goes further on production: it writes the article and opens the pull request.

**Can either tool find competitor gaps by engine?**
Otterly's gap analysis is split by engine ([Otterly gap analysis](https://otterly.ai/features/ai-search-content-gap-analysis)). Jam's gap analysis covers the platforms it tracks, and its plan lets you scan weekly, every other day or daily ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)).

**Does Jam have a free plan?**
Jam's pricing page has a "Start free" button but does not state a trial length or free tier limits ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)).

# Features

## Outlines
Fold, zoom and drag any branch. Turn a bullet into its own note with one shortcut.

## Backlinks
Type `[[` to link a note. Every note shows what links to it.

## Semantic search
Search by meaning. "that idea about onboarding emails" finds the note even if you never wrote those words.

## Offline sync
Quillmark stores everything locally first and syncs in the background with end-to-end encryption.

## Publishing
Publish any note as a public page with a custom domain on Pro.

## Platforms
Quillmark runs on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, and every version stores your notes on the device first.

- **macOS:** Open and edit your outlines on a Mac with no connection, and changes sync in the background with end-to-end encryption once you are back online.
- **Windows:** Notes are stored locally on your PC, so you can keep writing offline and your changes sync in the background when the connection returns.
- **iOS:** Your notes live on your iPhone or iPad first, so you can keep outlining offline and they sync with end-to-end encryption when you reconnect.
- **Android:** Notes are stored locally on your phone or tablet, so you can keep working offline and your changes sync in the background when you are online again.
